## Product name
IsNotAEngine

## Short description
Native animated wallpapers for Windows, a different one on each monitor, with very low resource use.

## Description
IsNotAEngine puts animated wallpapers behind your desktop icons, built to stay light: shaders drawn straight on the GPU, looping videos, HTML/WebGL pages and images, each on its own monitor.

**What it does**
• One wallpaper per monitor, with independent pause and quality.
• Built-in library: shaders (Aurora, Night Sky, Rain on Glass, Smoke, Plasma, Gyroid and more), seamless looping videos (Megacity, Tokyo Rain) and web pages (Matrix, terminal, clock, system dashboard).
• Import your own: .hlsl shaders, videos (mp4, webm, mkv...), images, HTML pages and .livewallpaper packages.
• Live tuning: speed, brightness, colors and more, plus one accent color for everything.
• Reacts to the audio that is playing, the clock and the mouse (parallax), when the wallpaper supports it.
• Playlist with automatic switching, a time-of-day schedule and a live screensaver.
• Create with AI: describe a wallpaper and the AI writes a shader, a page or a 4K still image (uses YOUR Claude, Gemini or OpenRouter key; optional).

**Built to stay out of the way**
• Pauses by itself when a window covers the screen, when you are on battery, or when a game or presentation is full screen.
• Performance mode: drops usage to the minimum while you game.
• Tray icon to switch wallpapers, pause, or open the Studio.

**Privacy**
No account, no data collection, no telemetry. Wallpapers run without internet access. The only network use is the optional "Create with AI" feature, with the user's own key.

**Requirements:** Windows 10 (2004) or later, 64-bit, Direct3D 11 GPU. Uses WebView2 (included in Windows 11).

## What's new
First public release: per-monitor wallpapers, Studio, shader/video/page import, performance mode and Create with AI.
